Symptoms of Pastorypia

NOTE that although certain symptoms seem to be common to nearly all sufferers, many of these symptoms are RARE, some even occurring in as few as ONE OR TWO CASES. The list is ordered from MOST common to LEAST common. If you experience an unreported symptom, please CONTACT US through the form on the "CONTACT" page.

  • The characteristic symptom: flickering white orbs at the periphery of your vision. If you have this, likely you have Pastorypia, sorry to say. Fortunately, the visual symptoms don't seem to progress, and don't impede everyday life for most sufferers. However, some find them bothersome. Like every symptom listed here, treatments are suggested on our treatment page.
  • Sluggishness
  • Brain fog
  • Losing one's train of thought
  • Irritability
  • Bruising more easily
  • Weakness
    • Feeling like you can't move for long without stress
    • Losing muscle mass
    • Feeling of heart/lungs being inadequate
  • Abnormality in appetite
    • Needing to eat much more or much less than usual
    • Cravings for something unusual or inedible
  • Skin discoloration
    • Yellowness around the eyes
    • Purple markings on any part of the body
  • Skin ulcers
  • Mood swings
  • Throbbing pain at the base of the neck
  • Wounds appearing on the body with no explanation
    • Wounds that look like burn marks
    • Wounds that look like cuts
